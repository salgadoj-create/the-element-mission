YOUR CLASS VOCABULARY

Interrogative words

  • ¿Quién es…? asks about one person; ¿Quiénes son…? asks about people in the plural.
  • Cuánto/a asks “how much.” Cuántos/as asks “how many.” Match gender and number when the word modifies a noun.
  • ¿Por qué? asks “why?” The answer may begin porque… (“because…”).
  • A dónde and adónde are both accepted spellings for “to where.” Dónde can also occur with movement verbs; prompts requesting “to where” explicitly ask for a form with a.
  • Read the English clue to choose the intended question. More than one Spanish question can fit an unqualified sentence.
