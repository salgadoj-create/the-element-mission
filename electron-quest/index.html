Three rules for placing electrons

Aufbau principleFill lower-energy orbitals before higher-energy orbitals. For atoms H–Ca, use 1s, 2s, 2p, 3s, 3p, then 4s.
Pauli exclusion principleEach orbital can contain at most two electrons. If there are two, they must have opposite spins: ↑↓.
Hund’s ruleFor equal-energy orbitals in the same sublevel, place one electron in each with parallel spins before pairing any electrons.

Read the configuration

2p4 means 4 electrons in the p sublevel of energy level 2. It does not mean 4 p orbitals.
