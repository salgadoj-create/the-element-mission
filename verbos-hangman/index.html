How to play

  1. Read the English meaning and guess the Spanish -ar infinitive.
  2. Tap a letter or use your keyboard. A repeated letter guess never costs another turn.
  3. A correct letter reveals all matching positions. A wrong letter adds one part to the drawing.
  4. Solve the verb before six incorrect guesses to earn 100 points.
  5. Optional: reveal one new letter. You may do this once per word. Solving with that hint earns 75 points.
  6. After a solved or missed word, read the answer and continue. No word repeats within a session.

Use the separate Ñ key for enseñar. There are no articles to enter. The notebook contains all answers; review it before playing if you want a memory challenge. Scores and resume progress stay on this device when browser storage is available.
