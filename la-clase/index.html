🏆 Last Student Standing

A teacher-led competition on one shared screen.

  1. Students write A, B, C, or D on paper or a mini-whiteboard. Everyone answers the same question before anyone reveals.
  2. Ask everyone to show their answers together. Enter each active student's answer below the question.
  3. Lock the answers to reveal the correct choice. One incorrect answer eliminates a student.
  4. If everyone is wrong, nobody is eliminated: play a new question. Continue ties until exactly one student remains.

This mode runs on the teacher’s screen. Student devices do not connect to it. Names stay in this browser session and are not uploaded.

Use unique names or add a last initial. Eliminated students can keep answering for practice.
