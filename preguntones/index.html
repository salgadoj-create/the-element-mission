MODEL ANSWER(S)

Other grammatically correct answers that meet the stated target may also earn credit. The teacher makes the final decision.

How to play

  1. Form 2–6 teams and choose Spanish team names.
  2. Begin with a 100-point clue. Read the statement and the category directions.
  3. Teams discuss. The first team to buzz or raise a hand answers with a Spanish question.
  4. The teacher selects that team and marks its answer correct or incorrect.
  5. A correct answer earns the clue value. An incorrect answer earns no points; other teams may steal. Each team has only one attempt per clue.
  6. The team that answers correctly chooses the next category and value. If nobody answers correctly, the teacher chooses.
  7. Rotate speakers within each team. Continue until all 25 clues are used. The highest score wins; equal highest scores are a tie.

For the interrogative categories, use the specific target printed below the statement. No automatic spelling or accent grading is used: students answer aloud and the teacher judges grammar and meaning.
