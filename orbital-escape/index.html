YOUR CHEMISTRY FIELD GUIDE

Think like an electron.

  • Electron count: neutral atoms have Z electrons. For ions, electrons = Z − signed charge. Na⁺: 11 − (+1) = 10; O²⁻: 8 − (−2) = 10.
  • Aufbau: fill lower-energy orbitals first. For the atoms in this game: 1s → 2s → 2p → 3s → 3p → 4s.
  • Pauli: one orbital holds at most two electrons, with opposite spins: [↑↓].
  • Hund: fill equal-energy orbitals singly with parallel spins before pairing. Nitrogen 2p³: [↑] [↑] [↑].
  • Capacity: s = 1 orbital / 2 electrons; p = 3 / 6; d = 5 / 10; f = 7 / 14.
  • Notation: 2p⁴ means four electrons in the 2p sublevel. Enter configurations as 1s2 2s2 2p4, with superscripts or with carets (1s^2).
  • Noble-gas core: use the immediately preceding noble gas. Na = [Ne] 3s¹; K = [Ar] 4s¹. Core electrons count too!

This game focuses on the first 20 neutral elements and selected main-group ions; transition-metal exceptions are outside its scope.
