Formula & concept reference

c = λν   ·   E = hν   ·   E = hc/λ

c = 3.00 × 10⁸ m/s in a vacuum
h = 6.626 × 10⁻³⁴ J·s
1 nm = 10⁻⁹ m (exact)

λ = wavelength (m); ν (Greek nu) = frequency (Hz); E = energy per photon (J). Convert nm to m before substituting. The speed c is constant in a vacuum; light travels more slowly in materials.

Shorter λ → higher ν → higher photon energy. Emission: electron moves down; absorption: electron moves up. Photon energy equals the energy gap.
